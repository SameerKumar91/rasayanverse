PRACTICAL 02

Determination of pH

Determine the pH of different solutions using a pH meter and classify them as acidic, neutral or basic.

⚗ Acid • Base ⏱ 10–15 min 🎓 Guided Practical
Key Concept
pH = −log[H⁺]
pH < 7 → Acidic   •   pH = 7 → Neutral   •   pH > 7 → Basic
Concept Aim Apparatus Chemicals Safety Procedure 🧪 Virtual Lab Result Quiz
01 • BASIC CONCEPT

What is pH?

pH is a measure of how acidic or basic a solution is. It is related to the concentration of hydrogen ions present in the solution.

The pH scale generally ranges from 0 to 14. A value below 7 indicates an acidic solution, 7 is neutral, and a value above 7 indicates a basic solution.

In this experiment, a digital pH meter is used to measure the pH of hydrochloric acid, distilled water and sodium hydroxide.

pH = −log[H⁺]

The pH value helps us identify the acidic, neutral or basic nature of a solution.

⚠
05 • SAFETY

Work Carefully

Handle acids and bases carefully.
Never taste or directly smell chemicals.
Rinse the pH electrode before testing another sample.
Keep the pH meter and electrical connections dry.

WHAT HAPPENS?

Why does the pH reading change?

Different solutions contain different concentrations of hydrogen ions. The pH electrode detects the chemical activity of the solution and sends a signal to the meter. The meter converts that signal into a pH value, allowing us to identify the solution as acidic, neutral or basic.
