Flame Test

Identify selected metal ions by observing the characteristic colour they produce in a flame.

⚗ Qualitative Analysis⏱ 8–10 min🎓 Guided Practical
Reaction / Principle
Metal Ion + Flame → Characteristic Flame Colour

01 • BASIC CONCEPT

What is a Flame Test?

A flame test is a qualitative analysis method used to identify certain metal ions.

When a metal salt is heated in a flame, electrons become excited. As they return to lower energy levels, they emit light of characteristic colour.

Different ions can therefore produce different flame colours.

Excitation → Emission of characteristic light

This principle is the key idea behind the practical.

🎯
AIM

To identify selected metal ions by observing their characteristic flame colours.

Why does each ion have a different colour?

The energy difference between electron levels is characteristic of each element. The emitted light therefore has characteristic wavelengths and colours.
