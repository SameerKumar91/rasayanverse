01 • BASIC CONCEPT

What are Acid–Base Indicators?

An acid–base indicator is a substance that changes colour when the acidity or basicity of a solution changes.

In this practical, you will test an acidic solution, distilled water and a basic solution using an indicator.

Each indicator has a characteristic colour in acidic and basic conditions. Comparing the colour helps us identify the nature of the solution.

Indicator + solution → characteristic colour

This principle is the key idea behind the practical.

🎯
AIM

WHAT HAPPENS?

Why does the indicator change colour?

Indicators contain molecules whose structure changes with the concentration of hydrogen ions. This produces different colours in different pH ranges.
