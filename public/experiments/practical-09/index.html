PRACTICAL 09

Chloride Ion Test

Test a solution for chloride ions using silver nitrate and observe the characteristic white precipitate.

⚗ Qualitative Analysis⏱ 8–10 min🎓 Guided Practical
Reaction / Principle
Cl⁻ + AgNO₃ → AgCl↓ + NO₃⁻
A white precipitate of silver chloride indicates chloride ions.
ConceptAimApparatusChemicalsSafetyProcedure🧪 Virtual LabResultQuiz
01 • BASIC CONCEPT

How is Chloride Detected?

Chloride ions can be tested by adding silver nitrate solution to an appropriately prepared sample.

If chloride ions are present, insoluble silver chloride forms as a white precipitate.

The precipitate provides a visible qualitative indication of chloride.

Ag⁺ + Cl⁻ → AgCl↓

This principle is the key idea behind the practical.

🎯
AIM

To identify chloride ions in a given solution using silver nitrate solution.

WHAT HAPPENS?

Why does a white precipitate appear?

Silver ions combine with chloride ions to form silver chloride, which is sparingly soluble in water and therefore separates as a solid precipitate.
