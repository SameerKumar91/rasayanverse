PRACTICAL 13

Redox Titration

Perform a guided redox titration and identify the endpoint from the characteristic colour change of permanganate.

⚗ Redox Chemistry⏱ 10–15 min🎓 Guided Practical
Reaction / Principle
MnO₄⁻ + Reducing Agent → Mn²⁺ + Oxidized Product
Permanganate can act as a self-indicating titrant in acidic medium.
ConceptAimApparatusChemicalsSafetyProcedure🧪 Virtual LabResultQuiz
01 • BASIC CONCEPT

What is a Redox Titration?

A redox titration determines the amount of a substance using an oxidation–reduction reaction.

In a common permanganate titration, potassium permanganate acts as an oxidizing agent in acidic medium.

The endpoint can be observed when a faint pink colour persists because a slight excess of permanganate remains.

Oxidation + Reduction → Redox reaction

This principle is the key idea behind the practical.

🎯

WHAT HAPPENS?

Why can permanganate indicate the endpoint?

Permanganate is strongly coloured. Before the endpoint it is consumed by the reducing agent; at a slight excess, its pink colour persists.
