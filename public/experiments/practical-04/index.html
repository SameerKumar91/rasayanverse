Preparation of Standard Solution

Prepare a solution of known concentration by accurately measuring solute and making the solution up to a fixed volume.

⚗ Solutions • Measurement⏱ 10–12 min🎓 Guided Practical
Reaction / Principle
Solute + Solvent → Standard Solution

01 • BASIC CONCEPT

What is a Standard Solution?

A standard solution has a accurately known concentration and is used in quantitative chemical analysis.

The solute is first measured accurately, dissolved in a small amount of distilled water and transferred to a volumetric flask.

The flask is then filled carefully up to the calibration mark and mixed thoroughly.

M = m / (Mᵣ × V)

This principle is the key idea behind the practical.

WHAT HAPPENS?

Why is the calibration mark important?

The calibration mark defines an accurate final volume. Combining the exact amount of solute with this volume gives the intended concentration.
