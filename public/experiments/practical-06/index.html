PRACTICAL 06

Crystallization of Copper Sulphate

Obtain copper sulphate crystals by concentrating a solution and allowing it to cool slowly.

⚗ Separation • Solubility⏱ 12–15 min🎓 Guided Practical
Reaction / Principle
CuSO₄ Solution → Concentration → Cooling → CuSO₄ Crystals
Crystals form when a hot saturated solution cools and solubility decreases.
ConceptAimApparatusChemicalsSafetyProcedure🧪 Virtual LabResultQuiz
01 • BASIC CONCEPT

What is Crystallization?

Crystallization is a purification technique used to obtain a solid in a crystalline form from a solution.

A copper sulphate solution is concentrated by gentle heating. On cooling, the solubility decreases and crystals begin to separate.

Slow cooling helps produce better-formed crystals.

Hot concentrated solution → Cooling → Crystals

This principle is the key idea behind the practical.

WHAT HAPPENS?

Why do crystals form on cooling?

Copper sulphate is more soluble in hot water than cold water. As the concentrated solution cools, excess solute separates as crystals.
